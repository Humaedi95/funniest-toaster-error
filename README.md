A single deadpan error message from a kitchen toaster that has just lost its bread: the slice slipped out of the slot mid-cycle, and the appliance reports the departure the way a hotel front desk reports a guest who checked out without a word. Running `python3 main.py` writes the one-line message to `/output/entry.txt`.
